  • COMORBUSS: optimized interventions against COVID-19 on complex networks2020

    An agent-based model of how a disease spreads through a community's social contacts, used to compare containment policies in cities with different structures.

  • How user information drives Amazon's product recommendations2021

    Course project in applied economics at Télécom Paris, with D. J. Deutsch. We simulated users with different browsing histories, plus a control user with none, and compared their search results. We found strong signs of price steering but no significant price discrimination.
